Create Your Website

As at 03/12/2024:

Step 2: Log in with your Gmail account.

Step 3: Start a new site
We shall choose a template from the Template gallery


(a.) Choose whatever template you want.
For this session, let us select the Class template from the Education category


The template is known as your Worksite or the Website Editor.


(b.) Let us change the Class Name.
The choice of font, font size, and class name is yours.


(c.) Let us add a logo and a favicon

One of the benefits of using Google Sites is that all changes are automatically updated in your Google Drive.
