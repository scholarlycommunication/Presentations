Today, we want to focus on a valuable use of artificial intelligence: Language Models.
One of such models is the Google Notebook Language Model also known as the Google Notebook LM
Please NOTE: As with all AI teaching and learning tools, please proofread/review any AI-generated content for accuracy.

The Google Notebook Language Model is an artificial intelligence research assistant that enables one to learn/understand/summarize and interact with the source(s) (contents) being uploaded to it.
Notice that Student—Content interaction is one of the features of the RSI (Regular and Substantive Interaction) requirements for distance education.

A source is a static copy of the resource one wants to learn/understand/summarize.
As at today: 03/21/2025, the supported source types are:
(1.) Webpages (from a website)
(2.) Portable Document Files (.pdf)
(3.) Text files (.txt)
(4.) Markdown files (.md)
(5.) Audio files
(6.) Copy-pasted text
(7.) YouTube URLs of public videos
(8.) Google Docs
(9.) Google Slides
Each source can contain up to 500,000 words, or up to 200MB for uploaded files.
